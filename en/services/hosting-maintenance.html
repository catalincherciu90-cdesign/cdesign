Frequently asked questions

Do I need a maintenance plan for my website?

If your website matters to your business, we recommend a maintenance plan. Software, plugins and themes need regular updates to stay secure and compatible. With a maintenance plan, everything is updated, backed up and monitored, so small problems are caught before they lead to downtime or security risks.

What does a monthly plan include?

Our monthly plans usually include managed hosting, CDN and SSL via Cloudflare, regular software and plugin updates, off-site backups, security monitoring, uptime checks and a set monthly amount of content changes and support. We tailor the plan to your site, so you only pay for what you need.

Can you host my existing website?

Yes. We can move most existing websites to our managed hosting, including those built on WordPress, WooCommerce and other well-known platforms. We handle the whole transfer, test everything on a staging copy and switch over with minimal or zero downtime, so your visitors won't even notice the move.

How often do you make backups?

As standard, we make an automatic daily backup of your files and database, stored securely on off-site servers and kept on rotation for a set period. For high-traffic websites or online shops we can increase the frequency, and we always take a fresh backup before any update or change, so we can restore the site quickly if needed.

What is your support response time?

Support requests are handled during business hours, Monday to Friday, between 9:00 and 18:00. We aim to reply to routine requests within one working day, while urgent issues, such as a site that has stopped working, take priority and are resolved as quickly as possible. You can reach us by phone on +40 753 116 155 or through the contact form.
