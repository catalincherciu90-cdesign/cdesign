Why AI and how it works

AI works best when it's tailored to your business and based on your real content. Here's how we approach a project.

1. We start with a real problem

We look at where you're losing time or leads — slow replies, repetitive admin, enquiries that arrive out of hours — and use AI for exactly that task, rather than adding unnecessary gimmicks.

2. We base it on your content

The assistant is trained on your pages, FAQs and documents, so its answers stay accurate and true to your brand. You decide exactly what information it can access.

3. We build it quickly and efficiently

We use trusted providers such as Groq and Cloudflare AI for fast, low-cost responses, with API keys kept safely on the server and GDPR in mind.

4. People stay in control

AI handles the routine tasks and passes anything sensitive on to you. Your customers get faster service, without you losing the personal touch.

Frequently asked questions

What can AI do for my business?

AI can answer customer questions on your website around the clock, draft content in your brand's style, capture and qualify leads and take over repetitive tasks such as tagging enquiries, routing messages and updating records. The goal is simple: faster answers for your customers and fewer hours of manual work for your team.

Is using AI expensive?

Usually not. We work with efficient providers such as Groq and Cloudflare AI, so for a small or medium-sized business the day-to-day costs are generally low and predictable. Before we build anything, we estimate your usage and give you a clear forecast, with no made-up figures.

Is my data safe if I use AI?

Yes. We only give the AI the information you approve, keep secrets and API keys on the server and work with trusted providers that make commitments on data protection. We keep GDPR in mind and can avoid sending personal data to the model where it isn't needed.

Can AI use my own content?

Yes. We can base the assistant on your website pages, documents, FAQs and product information, so it answers using your real content and brand voice, not generic guesses. You decide what information it can access.

How long does it take to add AI to my website?

It depends on what the AI assistant needs to do and how many tools we connect. We'll confirm a realistic timeline once we understand your goals and systems.
