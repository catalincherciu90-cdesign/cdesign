Frequently asked questions

Why does it start from €400? What determines the final price?
The €400 price covers the basic package, with a simple 3-5 page website, a logo, domain, hosting and social media. The price can go up depending on the number of pages, special features (online shop, bookings etc.) or how complex the logo is. You'll get the exact price after the free consultation.
How long does it take to deliver the complete package?
It depends on how quickly you send us the materials (text, photos). We agree on the deadline together before work starts, and we put it in the quote. We deliver the logo within the first 3-5 days.
What happens after the first year of hosting?
After the first year, you can renew the hosting and domain at the provider's standard price (usually €50-80/year). We'll let you know in good time, and you're under no obligation to stay with us.
Can I choose which social networks I want?
Yes. We usually create Facebook and Instagram accounts, but we can do any combination: TikTok, LinkedIn, Google Business etc. We choose together what's right for your business.
Can I add extra features later?
Of course. The website is built so it can grow with your business. Later on we can add an online shop, a blog, a booking system or anything else you need.
