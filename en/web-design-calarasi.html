Frequently asked questions

Questions about web design in Călărași

How much does a website for a Călărași business cost?

A business website starts from €400, with a .ro domain and hosting included for the first year. For an online shop or a website with many service pages, you get a fixed-price quote after a 30-minute call.

I have a farm or an agricultural business. Why would I need a website?

Buyers, partners and new suppliers look you up online before they call. A simple website showing what you produce, what services you offer and how they can contact you makes you easy to find and shows you’re a serious business.

Can I sell machinery or parts directly from the website?

Yes. We can build a catalogue with prices and quote requests, or an online shop with payment and delivery. We’ll recommend the right option once we see how many products you have and how you sell now.

Do you have an office in Călărași?

No. We work online with businesses all over Romania: we talk by video call or phone and send materials by email and WhatsApp. That’s how we keep the price low.

Will my website show up on Google for searches in Călărași?

We optimise it for local searches such as “your service + Călărași” and connect it to your Google Business Profile. Your ranking depends on the competition and can’t be guaranteed, but the technical side is done right from launch.
