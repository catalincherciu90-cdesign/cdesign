02What data we collect

We only collect the data you voluntarily enter in the booking/contact form on the website. Specifically, this is:

  • Name – so we know how to contact you and address you correctly;
  • Phone number – to confirm the appointment and discuss the details of your request;
  • Email address – for correspondence and to send you the quote or the confirmation;
  • Preferred date and time – to schedule the consultation;
  • Message / request details – the information you choose to include about your project (optional).

We do not ask for or collect sensitive data (such as data about health, political or religious opinions, etc.), and we do not ask for payment details through this form.

03Why we collect data

We use your data exclusively to:

  • respond to your requests for consultation and information;
  • confirm and manage appointments requested through the website;
  • contact you to discuss the details of the project and send you a quote.

We do not use your data for any other purpose and we never sell it to third parties.

04Legal basis

The processing of your personal data is based on your consent, given when you fill in and submit the booking form, in accordance with Article 6(1)(a) GDPR.

You can withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal. To do so, you can write to us at office@c-design.ro.

05How long we keep data

We keep your personal data for a period of 12 months from our last interaction with you (for example, the last message, call or appointment). Once this period has expired, the data is securely deleted or anonymised.

If you become a client and we enter into a contract, we may keep certain data for longer, to the extent required by legal obligations (for example, tax and accounting obligations). We will inform you separately in that case.

07Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights in relation to your personal data:

  • Right of access – to find out what data we hold about you and to receive a copy of it;
  • Right to rectification – to correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • Right to erasure (the “right to be forgotten”) – to ask us to delete your data;
  • Right to data portability – to receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format;
  • Right to restriction of processing – to ask us to limit how we use your data;
  • Right to object – to object to the processing of your data in certain situations.

To exercise any of these rights, you can write to us at office@c-design.ro. We will respond to your request within the time limits set by law (as a rule, within 30 days at most).

If you believe your rights have not been respected, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing (ANSPDCP) – www.dataprotection.ro.

08Cookies

Our website uses cookies (small files stored in your browser) to measure performance and assess the effectiveness of our campaigns. We use two categories of cookies, both non-essential to the basic functioning of the website:

  • Analytics cookies (Google Analytics 4) – help us understand, in aggregate, how the website is used (pages visited, duration, traffic source);
  • Marketing cookies (Meta Pixel) – used for remarketing and to measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns.

Legal basis and consent

These cookies are set exclusively on the basis of your consent, in accordance with Article 6(1)(a) GDPR. On your first visit we show a consent banner, and no analytics or marketing cookie is loaded and no tracking script runs until you explicitly click “Accept”. If you decline or ignore the banner, Google Analytics and Meta Pixel remain blocked, and the website remains fully functional.

How to withdraw your consent

You can withdraw or change your consent at any time, without having to justify your decision:

  • click the “Cookie settings” link in the footer of any page to reopen the consent banner and change your choices;
  • or delete and block cookies directly in your browser settings.

After withdrawal, analytics and marketing cookies are no longer set. Withdrawing your consent does not affect your access to the website’s content in any way and does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

Third-party providers and transfers outside the EU

The cookies above are set by third-party providers (Google and Meta), which act as independent controllers for the data collected through their own technologies. They may transfer and process data outside the European Union (for example, in the United States), on the basis of the transfer mechanisms provided for by the GDPR. How they process this data is governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Meta: facebook.com/privacy/policy
